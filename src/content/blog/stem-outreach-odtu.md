We hosted a robotics introductory workshop for 6th graders at our school. Over 40 students got their first taste of FTC game design and robot programming.

During the season, communication and documentation are just as important as building the robot. We use this blog to maintain a transparent record of our design process and to share our learnings with the wider FTC community.

### Inspiring the Next Generation

We see our true success in the young minds we inspire and the STEM ecosystem we cultivate. For us, robotics is not just about building robots—it is about thinking, producing, and inspiring.

> "We don't just build robots to compete; we build them to learn, to challenge ourselves, and to inspire others."

Stay tuned for more outreach updates throughout the season!
